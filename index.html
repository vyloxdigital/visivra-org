The VisiVra Formula

Six Clinically-Studied Nutrients Inside Every VisiVra Supplement Capsule

Each VisiVra ingredient is selected for its direct, peer-reviewed role in retinal health, macular protection, or ocular antioxidant defense.

🌻

Lutein in VisiVra

The primary carotenoid that forms your macula's protective pigment layer, Lutein acts as an internal blue-light filter — absorbing harmful high-energy wavelengths from digital screens before they reach the delicate photoreceptor cells beneath. Higher macular pigment density correlates with lower risk of age-related macular degeneration.

💡

Zeaxanthin in VisiVra

Lutein's synergistic partner, Zeaxanthin concentrates in the central fovea — the pinpoint zone responsible for sharp, high-resolution vision used in reading and facial recognition. Together, Lutein and Zeaxanthin in VisiVra rebuild the two-pigment macular shield that screens out oxidative stress throughout the day.

PubMed — Zeaxanthin & Macular Pigment ↗
🍮

Bilberry Extract in VisiVra

Rich in anthocyanins, Bilberry Extract enhances microcirculation in the choroidal blood vessels that supply the retina, accelerating nutrient and oxygen delivery to photoreceptor cells. Studies link Bilberry supplementation to improvements in night vision adaptation and reduced visual fatigue after prolonged computer use.

PubMed — Bilberry & Retinal Blood Flow ↗
🌎

Astaxanthin in VisiVra

Classified as the world's most potent antioxidant carotenoid, Astaxanthin crosses the blood-retinal barrier — a feat most antioxidants cannot achieve — placing its radical-scavenging activity precisely where oxidative damage accumulates in eye tissue. Clinical research shows Astaxanthin reduces ciliary muscle fatigue, the root cause of digital eye strain.

PubMed — Astaxanthin Eye Fatigue Study ↗
⚡

Zinc in VisiVra

The highest mineral concentration in the human eye is zinc, which supports the enzyme Alcohol Dehydrogenase — critical for converting Vitamin A into retinal, the light-sensitive pigment in rod photoreceptors responsible for low-light and peripheral vision. Zinc deficiency directly impairs night vision, making it a non-negotiable nutrient in the VisiVra formula.

NIH ODS — Zinc Reference ↗
💎

Vitamin A in VisiVra

The foundational vision vitamin, Vitamin A is the molecular precursor to Rhodopsin — the photopigment that makes night vision possible in rod cells. Beyond night vision, Vitamin A maintains the conjunctival goblet cells that secrete the aqueous layer of your tear film, defending against the dry, gritty eyes that afflict screen users throughout the working day.
