OPTIMIZATION NOTES
------------------
- All product images should use WebP format for best performance (Lighthouse 90+)
- Compress with cwebp or Squoosh targeting < 80 KB per bottle image
- Review avatars can be stock photos of diverse adults (ages 46–58)
- Guarantee badge should be circular/shield shape with "60 Day" text
- Maintain consistent lighting and clean white/ocean-blue background across product shots
- Do NOT use JPG or PNG for product images — WebP only for performance

COLOR REFERENCE
---------------

Teal accent        : #0fa8a8
White background   : #ffffff
Text dark          : #0a1f33
